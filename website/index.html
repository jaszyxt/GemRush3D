The game

Weather is memory,
and memory is level design.

Every realm is a feeling, and every feeling is one new way for the sky to move you. Soft shapes, soft light, and platforming that forgives.

Weather plays with you

Tailwind gusts carry you over impossible gaps, updrafts lift you home, and a certain baby cloud giggles half a second before every blow.

Guardians, not enemies

The red spinners wake only when you linger too long. Nobody here is a villain — some of them just need to be asked nicely.

Playing plays the music

Gems climb a melody as you chain them, gusts land on the beat, and every star goes ding exactly where it should. The game is the soundtrack.

Falling upward

Coyote time, jump buffering, squash-and-stretch — precision where it counts, forgiveness everywhere else. Losing is just taking a nap.

Stars, medals & best times

Every level hides a full gem run. Sweep them all for three stars, chase the gold medal, then race your own best time.

Gloomfang’s Gifts

One hidden golden gem waits somewhere unlocked, every day. Miss a day and nothing bad happens — ever.

Touch or keyboard

A floating joystick appears only on touch screens; desktop gets crisp WASD and Space. Left-handed mode, bigger text, and a lefty-friendly jump button included.

A cozy promise

No ads, no energy meters, no streaks, no guilt-trip notifications. Comfort settings for shake, haptics and shadows — comfort is a feature, not a menu afterthought.

The sound

A soundtrack that doesn’t exist

Gem Rush 3D ships with zero audio files. Every note you hear — the chimes, the wind, the bells, the rain — is synthesized live on your device the moment you press play.

Positive events ring in C-major pentatonic, so chaining gems literally composes a riff, and gusts land on chord boundaries. The heart pickup sounds, in the composer’s notes, like “a hug in C.”

Questions

Asked nicely, answered kindly

Is Gem Rush 3D really free?

Yes. No ads, no in-app purchases, no energy meters, no paid anything. You download it, you play it, and the only thing it asks of you is to have a nice time.

What do I need to run it?

An Android phone or tablet with touch controls, or a Windows PC with a keyboard. The game plays in landscape at a smooth frame rate, and everything on screen is generated live — there’s nothing heavy to load.

Is it good for kids?

It’s built for them — warm, wry, and gentle. Hazards are “guardians”, running out of lives is “taking a nap”, and the whole game passes a photosensitivity audit: no flashing strobes, ever. The design test for every part of it: would a six-year-old who just lost feel invited back?

What are Gloomfang’s Gifts?

Once a day, a single hidden golden gem appears somewhere in the levels you’ve unlocked. Find it or don’t — miss a day and nothing bad happens, ever. It’s a small reason to smile, not a chore.

Any hints?

Guardians wake only when you linger — keep moving or tiptoe away. Nim giggles half a second before each gust, so listen. The goal portal hums before you can see it. And the checkpoints? They tell the story.
