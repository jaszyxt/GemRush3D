Description

Short

Gem Rush 3D is a cozy 3D platformer about a very small hero and a very large storm. Hop floating sky islands, reclaim the Sunstones that Gloomfang swallowed out of fear of the dark, and befriend the weather along the way — because nobody here is ever really a villain.

Long

When Gloomfang — a storm with abandonment issues — swallows the sky realm’s Sunstones, the Sky-Keeper’s little helper sets out to take the light back. Pip runs, hops and bounce-pads across floating islands where weather is the level design: tailwind gusts timed to the music, updrafts, sleeping guardians, echo bells that build bridges while they ring, and mirror doors into glassy skies.

Every realm in the Sky-Keeper’s atlas has its own sky and one new idea to master — and the realm keeps growing, with new realms already written. Somewhere past the first portals, the story stops being about retrieving light and becomes about handing it back: the redemption of a lonely storm, told in checkpoint-sized story beats.

And the sound? The game ships with zero audio files. Every chime, gust and bell is synthesized live on the device, tuned so that playing well literally plays music in C-major pentatonic.

History

Gem Rush 3D is built on one unusual rule: zero imported assets. Every platform, gem, cloud, character and even the app icon is painted by code at runtime, and the entire soundtrack is synthesized from raw samples the moment the game boots. The website you are reading follows the same spirit — hand-coded, no frameworks, no tracking.

Features

  • Precision platforming with forgiving feel — coyote time, jump buffering, squash-and-stretch
  • Weather as level design: gusts, updrafts, bounce pads, echo bells, mirror doors
  • Guardians instead of enemies — sleeping spinners wake only when you linger
  • A hidden realm where you play as the storm himself: no gravity, hold jump to rise
  • Hidden night-remix B-Sides: same bones, different sky
  • Stars and medals on every level, best-time chasing, story-telling checkpoints
  • Gloomfang’s Gifts: one hidden golden gem a day — miss a day, nothing bad happens, ever
  • Comfort options: screen shake, haptics, shadows, left-handed controls, larger text
  • Photosensitivity-audited: no strobing, ever
  • 100% synthesized soundtrack — zero audio files

Design promises

The game’s design doc is explicit about what it will never do: “Deliberately not doing: energy meters, loss-based streaks, ads, pop-ups, push-notification guilt.” The bar for every part of the game: would a six-year-old who just lost feel invited back?
